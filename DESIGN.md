# CleanLink Showcase Design System

## Direction

South African editorial campaign. The website should feel made by a team that understands the people and places behind the cleaning problem: locally grounded photography, practical language, lively colour, tactile labels and a few memorable scroll moments. Depth comes from overlapping photography, parallax and pinned narrative sequences—not ornamental 3D models.

## Typography

- Instrument Serif regular and italic for large, expressive editorial headlines.
- Manrope 400–700 for natural, readable explanation.
- DM Mono 400–500 for small labels, dates and workflow states.
- Paragraphs remain under 65 characters where practical.

## Colour

- Bottle green `#10251c`
- Soft green `#173127`
- Warm cream `#fff6df`
- Paper `#f3ead1`
- Soap mint `#79dda2`
- South African sun `#f6ca4b`
- Protea coral `#ef7966`
- Pool blue `#68c8ee`
- Jacaranda lilac `#b88ad8`
- Marigold `#ff9f43`

Colour changes carry meaning through the workflow. Dark green and cream keep the overall identity controlled while the six system stages move through the brighter palette.

## Pages

- Home: photo-led introduction, honest short explanation, long sticky scroll story and route index.
- Our Story: company context, physical-work photo break and fragmented-information problem.
- The System: six-stage pinned scroll workflow, role handover, product mockups and scope.
- The Team: three deliberate profile placeholders awaiting real team information.

## Motion and Depth

- Photos drift at different rates as they cross the viewport.
- The home story pins in place while text chapters change and a tinted layer wipes away.
- The System page pins a full-height journey and hands the viewer through six colour-coded stages, with photo, type and progress all changing in sequence.
- Hero image and copy move on separate depth planes during the first scroll.
- Interaction uses springs and transform/opacity only. Reduced motion collapses the effects.

## Photography

- Use South African context photography from Pexels and show its place, photographer and source on each image.
- Stock photography illustrates the project context only. It must never be presented as the sponsor’s actual staff or as the student team.
- Replace the Team-page contextual hero when genuine project photographs arrive.

## Accessibility

- Strong cream/ink contrast, visible focus rings and at least 44px interactive targets.
- The six workflow steps remain readable as a normal vertical sequence on narrow screens and when reduced motion is enabled.
- Images carry literal alternative text; decorative shapes remain hidden.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected.

## Content Rules

- Prefer ordinary, specific wording to slogans and promotional claims.
- Present CleanLink as a focused academic project, separate from the working product.
- Keep research sources internal and future ideas clearly separate from current scope.
- The team has exactly three members until the user supplies their details.
